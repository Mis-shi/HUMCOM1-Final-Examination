Space

📏 Cage Size

Pick a cage with lots of open floor space, at least 450 square inches, and secure latches. Bigger is always better.

Burrowing

🛏️ Bedding

Use 6 inches or more of paper-based or aspen bedding so your hamster can dig. Avoid cedar and pine shavings.

Exercise

🎡 Wheel & Play

Choose a solid wheel, about 8 inches for dwarfs and 10–12 inches for Syrians. Add tunnels, chew toys, and a hideout.

Daily care

🧼 Cleaning & Comfort

Spot-clean the bathroom corner daily and deep-clean every 2–4 weeks. Keep the room at 65–75°F (18–24°C), away from sun and drafts.
