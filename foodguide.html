Food Guide

Go ahead

✅ Safe Foods

  • 🥕 Carrots
  • 🥦 Broccoli
  • 🥒 Cucumber
  • 🍎 Apple (no seeds)
  • 🍓 Strawberry
  • 🥚 Plain cooked egg
  • 🌾 Plain oats
  • 🐛 Dried mealworms

Offer fresh foods in small pieces and only in moderation.

Keep away

⛔ Unsafe Foods

  • 🍫 Chocolate
  • 🧅 Onion & garlic
  • 🍊 Citrus fruits
  • 🥑 Avocado
  • 🌰 Raw almonds
  • 🍬 Candy & sugary snacks
  • 🍎 Apple seeds
  • 🧂 Salty snacks

When unsure about a food, do not feed it. Ask first.

Every day

🍽️ Feeding Tips

Daily staple

Quality hamster seed or pellet mix, about 1–2 tablespoons for Syrians and less for dwarfs.

Fresh treats

A small piece of veggie 2–3 times a week. Remove leftovers after a day.

Fresh water

Change it every day, using a bottle with a metal spout.

Food stash

Hamsters hoard food, so check their hideout and remove anything spoiled.
